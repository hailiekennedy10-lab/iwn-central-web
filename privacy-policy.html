Privacy Policy

IWN Central · Impact Wellness Network

Effective date: July 22, 2026 · Last updated: July 22, 2026

IWN Central ("the App") is a private, internal workforce application operated by Impact Wellness Network ("IWN," "we," "us," or "our") for use by our authorized staff at our treatment facilities. This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and how it is protected.

The App is not intended for the general public or for patients/clients. Access is limited to IWN staff whose accounts are created by an administrator. There is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, work email address, assigned role(s), assigned facility/site, work schedule, and (optionally) a profile photo you choose to upload. Passwords are handled securely by our authentication provider and are not visible to us in plain text.
  • Operational data you enter or that is assigned to you: shift records, task sheets and completions, checklists and their submissions, shift reports, staff evaluations and feedback, training progress, help requests, and related notes and timestamps.
  • Care-logistics references (non-identifying): to coordinate work, the App stores only non-identifying operational references — such as room/bed numbers, times, and auto-generated slot numbers (e.g. “Intake 1”) — together with task status. It does not store client names, initials, medical record numbers, or any clinical/treatment records. Client identity is maintained only in the facility's separate electronic medical record (EMR) system.
  • Device & technical data: a push-notification token (if you enable notifications) and activity/audit logs recording actions taken in the App for security and compliance.

How we use information

  • To operate the App: authenticate you, show your dashboard, assign and track shifts, tasks, checklists, and client-care logistics.
  • To send operational push notifications (for example, new assignments or reviews) when enabled.
  • For quality and supervision: shift reviews, staff evaluations, and training.
  • For security, integrity, and regulatory compliance: access control and audit logging.

How information is stored and shared

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing. Information is stored in our secure cloud backend and processed only by service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (e.g., profile photos).
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications (when enabled).

These providers process information solely to provide their services to us, under agreements that require appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Health information & HIPAA

IWN Central is a staff operations tool designed not to store protected health information (PHI). It records only non-identifying operational references (room/bed numbers, times, and slot numbers) and task status — not client names, initials, medical record numbers, or clinical records. Client identity and clinical documentation remain solely in IWN's separate electronic medical record (EMR) system, which is governed by IWN's HIPAA policies and safeguards.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the App and to meet IWN's operational, legal, and regulatory obligations. Staff accounts are deactivated or removed when a person leaves IWN or no longer requires access.

Security

We protect information using industry-standard measures, including encryption of data in transit, authenticated access, role- and site-based access controls, and audit logging. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Your choices

  • You can view and update your name, profile photo, work schedule, and notification preference within the App.
  • Your account is managed by an IWN administrator. To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact your administrator or use the contact below.

Children

The App is a workforce tool intended for adult IWN staff and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to staff, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
